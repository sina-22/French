-- grammer ----------------------------------------------

Some french verbs can be followed by "a"
when expressed in connection with an object

examples: 
    - Elle parle a Antoine 
    - Le concert plait a Jean


indirect objective pronouns: 
je          - me
tu          - te
il/elle     - lui
